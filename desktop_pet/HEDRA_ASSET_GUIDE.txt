HEDRA — LUMINA LLAMA ASSET GUIDE
================================

La V1 usa temporalmente el mismo icono geométrico de llama que ya tiene Lumina.
La siguiente fase consiste en sustituir sus frames por un personaje generado
con Hedra.

OBJETIVO VISUAL
---------------
A compact desktop mascot for a local AI study assistant called Lumina.
A stylized llama, elegant rather than childish, designed for an Engineering
Physics student. Futuristic academic aesthetic, cyan/ice-blue glow accents,
dark navy details, friendly intelligent expression, recognizable silhouette,
minimal visual clutter, premium software mascot design.

The llama should feel:
- intelligent
- curious
- calm
- technical
- approachable
- not childish
- not photorealistic

Avoid:
- text
- logos
- background scenery
- excessive accessories
- cartoon baby proportions
- photorealism

ASSET MASTER
------------
Generate a clean isolated full-body or 3/4-body character on a plain/transparent
background, facing slightly left, with enough negative space around ears and legs.

Recommended master:
- square 1024x1024 or larger
- transparent background if Hedra supports it
- centered character
- no shadows extending far outside the character

ANIMACIONES QUE QUEREMOS
------------------------
1. idle_loop
   - 2–4 seconds
   - subtle breathing / body movement
   - occasional ear movement
   - seamless loop

2. blink
   - very short natural blink
   - otherwise identical pose

3. hover
   - looks toward the cursor / slightly raises ears
   - subtle cyan glow

4. click
   - short acknowledgement
   - tiny head movement or nod
   - no camera movement

5. thinking (future)
   - focused expression
   - subtle pulsing glow

6. success (future)
   - small pleased motion

EXPORT
------
For desktop integration, export frames with:
- transparent background when possible
- same canvas size for every frame
- no camera movement
- character always in same approximate position
